# Match-up grid

Rows pairing "what you have" on the left with "what may fund it" on the right. Stacks vertically on mobile.

- **Where it goes:** in place of a bullet list that maps assets or needs to funding types.
- **Text it needs:** per row, a short label, a small sub-label and a one-line answer. Link answers to fundingbay.co.uk product pages where they exist.
- **JavaScript:** none.
