In short
  • Start with the deal, not the product. Split the price into goodwill, assets, property, fees, working capital and a cash buffer. Each part may need a different funding source.
  • Seven routes are available: senior debt, asset-backed lending, asset finance, vendor finance, equity, MBO/MBI funding and blended structures.
  • Match the money to what you are buying. Goodwill, debtors, equipment and property each point to a different type of funding.
  • Stress-test before you commit. A smaller debt burden can protect cash flow during integration.
